About Fatty's

One guy. Good meds. Fast.

Fatty's is a one-man medical delivery service based in Biddeford. The menu comes from cultivators all over Maine, picked for resin quality and clean, organic growing, at prices that make sense.

You're dealing with the person who builds the menu and drives the car. Same-day orders go out ASAP, and when you text, you get an answer.

Serving qualified medical patients only.

The deal

  • Same day in Biddeford, Saco & Old Orchard Beach

Questions

Good to know

Who can order?

Qualified medical cannabis patients 18 and older with a valid medical card or certification. You'll show it with a matching photo ID when your order arrives.

How do I order?

Check the menu, then send a delivery request with what you want. We'll text you to confirm the total and your delivery time. You can also text (207) 281-5302 or order on Weedmaps.

Do I pay online?

No. Nothing is charged online. You pay when your order arrives.

Is there a minimum?

Orders of $40 or more get free delivery.

How fast is delivery?

Same-day orders in Biddeford, Saco and OOB go out ASAP. Other towns depend on that day's route.

What are your hours?

Deliveries run 9am–7pm. Pre-orders are taken anytime, and we're closed major holidays.

I'm visiting from out of state. Can I order?

Reach out 24 hours ahead and we'll meet at the Biddeford Park & Ride.
