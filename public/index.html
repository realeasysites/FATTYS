Who can order?

Qualified medical cannabis patients 18 and older with a valid medical card or certification. You'll show it with a matching photo ID when your order arrives.

How do I order?

Check the menu, then send a delivery request with what you want. We'll text you to confirm the total and your delivery time. You can also just text your order, or order on Weedmaps.

Do I pay online?

No. Nothing is charged online. You pay when your order arrives.

Is there a minimum?

Orders of $40 or more get free delivery.

How fast is delivery?

Same-day orders in Biddeford, Saco and OOB go out ASAP. Other towns depend on that day's route.

What are your hours?

Deliveries run 9am–7pm. Pre-orders are taken anytime, and we're closed major holidays.

Visiting from out of state?

Reach out 24 hours ahead and we'll meet at the Biddeford Park & Ride.
